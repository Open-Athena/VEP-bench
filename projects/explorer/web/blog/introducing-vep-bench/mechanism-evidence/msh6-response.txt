## Interpretation and numerical scale

These are **sequence-based point predictions**, not measurements. The strongest conclusions concern changes to the splice-site dinucleotides and their immediate recognition sequences. Predictions involving branchpoint choice, cryptic splice sites, or exonic regulatory motifs are less certain.

The absolute percentage-point scale is especially uncertain because wild-type PSI is not supplied. I use large negative values for likely near-loss-of-function alleles, intermediate negatives for weakened sites with plausible rescue, and small changes for unvalidated regulatory-motif effects.

A consequential assay ambiguity is how **boundary-shifted inclusion products** were counted. I allow plausible nearby splice sites to reduce the predicted severity of a lesion. If the PSI calculation counts only the exact wild-type junctions, several rescue-based predictions below would be substantially more negative.

All deletion intervals below describe the bases actually removed, **excluding the retained VCF anchor**.

## Relevant sequence features

### Acceptor and upstream intron

The tested exon begins at 217. Its wild-type acceptor is:

```text
201–213        214–216   217 onward
TTTTTTTTTTTTT  AAG       GTGAAAG...
                  AG | G
```

Important features are:

- **AG215–216**, the canonical acceptor dinucleotide.
- **T201–213**, a 13-nt uninterrupted polypyrimidine tract.
- **A193**, in the branchpoint-like sequence `TTCAT` at 190–194, 23 nt before acceptor G216. This is a plausible branchpoint, **not an experimentally established one**.
- Other possible branchpoint contexts remain upstream. These include `GTAAT` at 166–170, potentially branching at A169, and a weaker context around A174.
- The complete cassette also contains `TACTAAC` at **142–148**, with a strong-looking branchpoint context around A147. It is relatively distant from the wild-type acceptor, but becomes closer after upstream deletions.

Potential downstream acceptor dinucleotides include:

- **AG222–223**, which could produce an exon beginning at 224.
- **AG243–244**, which could produce an exon beginning at 245.

Their presence does not prove use, but they are important alternatives when the normal acceptor is damaged.

### Donor

The wild-type donor is:

```text
304–306 | 307–312
   TAG  | GTAAGA
```

The essential intronic **GT307–308** is intact in wild type. The site also has favorable +3 A, +4 A, and +5 G, although exonic −3 T and intronic +6 A are not optimal consensus bases. Thus, both the terminal exonic bases and intronic positions +3 through +6 can materially affect recognition.

### Internal regulatory sequences

Several internal sequences support weaker, testable regulatory hypotheses:

- Purine-rich `GAAAG` at **219–223** and `AAGTGAAA` at **242–249** could contribute enhancer activity.
- `CCAG` at **253–256** matches a short SRSF2-like enhancer consensus. Such a short match is not proof of binding.
- `TGCATG` at **265–270**, or RNA `UGCAUG`, is a recognizable **RBFOX-family binding motif**. Exonic RBFOX binding can repress inclusion, although activity here depends on factor expression and occupancy in HEK293T cells.
- `TCTCT` at **282–286**, or RNA `UCUCU`, is a plausible **PTBP-family-associated silencer motif**.

The complete upstream and downstream introns are **154 nt and 132 nt**, respectively. Consequently, the intronic deletions should not be penalized simply because the *native* flanks become short: the fixed reporter introns remain part of the substrates.

## What would resolve the main uncertainties?

- **Measured wild-type PSI** is needed to calibrate the absolute percentage-point scale and its floor/ceiling.
- **Junction-resolved RT-PCR or RNA sequencing** is needed to test the proposed acceptor and donor shifts, particularly for V10–V20 and the donor-spanning deletions.
- **Branchpoint mapping** would distinguish use of A193 from upstream alternatives and substantially improve predictions for V02, V03, V07, and V09.
- **Targeted motif substitutions, factor perturbation, or binding measurements in HEK293T cells** would test the enhancer/silencer hypotheses for V22–V31.
- Zero-valued predictions mean **no defensible directional effect from the supplied sequence**, not experimentally demonstrated neutrality.
